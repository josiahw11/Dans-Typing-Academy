Typing 101

The rules, in large print

  1. Sit up straight. We know about your back. Do it anyway.
  2. Home row. Left fingers on ASDF, right fingers on JKL;. Thumbs rest on the space bar.
  3. Feel the bumps on F and J. That's how you find home without looking. Like a light switch in the dark.
  4. Each finger owns its keys. The glowing colors show who owns what. No trespassing.
  5. Reach, then return. After every key, that finger goes back home. Every time.
  6. Eyes on the screen. Not the keys. The keys aren't going anywhere.
  7. Accuracy first. Speed shows up on its own. Like the grandkids when they want something.
